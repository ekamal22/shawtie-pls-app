Shawtie pls Privacy Notice

Policy version: 2026-10-03

Shawtie pls is a private two-person communication and relationship application. This notice describes the product data flows intended for the first stable release.

Data we process

Account data includes your verified email, username, display name, date of birth, device records, authentication and security metadata, notification preferences, partnership state, and information needed to operate account recovery and deletion.

Operational metadata may include IP-derived security identifiers, timestamps, delivery state, connection state, call timing, storage identifiers, and bounded security or reliability logs.

Protected content

Protected messages, protected relationship content, and protected media use the application's reviewed end-to-end encryption design. Shawtie servers are not intended to possess the plaintext needed to read that protected content. Call media is encrypted in transit between endpoints and is not recorded by the first stable release.

End-to-end encryption does not hide all metadata. Infrastructure can still process information needed to route, secure, deliver, and delete data.

Service providers

Shawtie may use infrastructure providers for hosting, databases, private object storage, TURN relay, Web Push delivery, transactional email, backups, and operational monitoring. Providers receive only the information required for their function. Transactional email providers necessarily receive the destination email and the minimal email content sent to that address. Push providers receive device routing material and minimal notification metadata.

Notification privacy

Message plaintext is not sent to the push provider. Notification previews are hidden by default. If you enable notification details, Shawtie may identify the notification category, but protected message content remains excluded from the push payload.

Account and partnership deletion

Account deletion begins with a seven-day recovery period during which normal account access is removed. Partnership dissolution and account deletion follow the lifecycle and deletion rules shown in the application. Shawtie deletes application-controlled private content according to those rules. Shawtie cannot delete screenshots, exports, recordings, or other copies retained outside Shawtie by another person.

Backups and security records

Production backups use bounded retention and are required to preserve product deletion obligations when restored. Limited security or lifecycle evidence may be retained for a bounded period where needed to operate and secure the service.

Abuse and support

You can submit abuse or account-support reports from Shawtie. Because protected content is end-to-end encrypted, support personnel may be unable to inspect message or media plaintext. Do not include private message content or recovery secrets in a support report unless a support flow explicitly requests it.

Your choices

You can manage your profile, devices, notification privacy, partnership lifecycle, and account deletion through the application subject to the product rules. Some presence and read-receipt behavior is part of the shared product contract and is not optional.

Changes

If this notice changes materially, Shawtie will version the notice and require new acceptance when appropriate.
